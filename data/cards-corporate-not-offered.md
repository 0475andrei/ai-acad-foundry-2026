To avoid confusion with other banks' offerings, this document lists retail
products that Libra Bank does **not** currently offer:

- **Student loans.** Libra Bank does not offer dedicated student loan
  products. Students needing financing are directed to the Personal Loan
  product, subject to standard eligibility criteria, or to government
  student financing programs outside Libra Bank.
- **Corporate credit cards.** Libra Bank's card products described in this
  corpus (Libra Debit, Libra Credit Classic, Libra Credit Gold) are retail
  products only. Business and corporate cards are handled by a separate
  Libra Bank Business division and are out of scope for this assistant.
- **Cryptocurrency accounts or wallets.** Libra Bank does not provide
  custody, trading, or accounts denominated in cryptocurrency.
- **Foreign currency mortgages.** All Libra Bank mortgages are issued in RON
  only; the bank does not offer EUR- or USD-denominated mortgage loans to
  retail customers.

If a customer asks about any of the above, the correct answer is that Libra
Bank does not offer that product, not a best-effort guess based on similar
products.
