title: Card Blocking and Unblocking Procedure
product: cards
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15

A card is blocked automatically after three consecutive failed PIN attempts,
whether at an ATM, a POS terminal, or during online verification. A card can
also be blocked manually by the customer at any time.

**To block a card manually:**

1. Open the Libra Bank mobile app and go to the Cards section.
2. Select the card to block and tap "Block card temporarily."
3. Confirm the action with your app PIN or biometric login.
4. The card is blocked within seconds; a confirmation notification is sent
   by SMS and push notification.
5. Alternatively, call the 24/7 card support line at 0800 123 456 and confirm
   your identity with your account number and date of birth.

**To block a card permanently (lost or stolen):**

1. Follow steps 1-3 above, but select "Report lost or stolen" instead of
   "Block temporarily."
2. Confirm you want a permanent block; this cannot be undone through
   self-service.
3. A replacement card is automatically ordered and arrives within 5-7
   business days, or can be collected the next business day at any branch
   for a 15 RON express fee.

**To unblock a temporarily blocked card:**

1. Open the Libra Bank mobile app, go to Cards, and select the blocked card.
2. Tap "Unblock card."
3. If the card was blocked due to failed PIN attempts, you will be asked to
   confirm a new PIN before the card is reactivated.
4. Unblocking takes effect within seconds. If the app shows an error,
   contact card support, since some fraud-related blocks require manual
   review before they can be lifted.

A card that has been blocked due to suspected fraud cannot be unblocked
through the app; it must be reviewed by the fraud team, who will contact the
customer directly.
