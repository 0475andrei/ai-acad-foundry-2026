Libra Bank handles all retail customer complaints through the following
process.

1. **Submit the complaint.** Customers can submit a complaint through the
   mobile app (Support > File a complaint), by calling 0800 123 456, by
   email to reclamatii@librabank.ro, or in person at any branch.
2. **Acknowledgement.** Libra Bank sends a written acknowledgement within 2
   business days, including a reference number the customer can use to
   track the complaint.
3. **Investigation.** The relevant department investigates the complaint.
   Simple complaints (for example, a disputed fee) are typically resolved
   within 10 business days. Complex complaints (for example, disputed
   transactions requiring coordination with a card network) can take up to
   30 calendar days, as allowed under Romanian banking regulation.
4. **Resolution.** The customer receives a written response explaining the
   outcome and, if applicable, any correction made to their account (fee
   refund, transaction reversal, and so on).
5. **Escalation.** If the customer is not satisfied with the response, they
   can request escalation to Libra Bank's internal Ombudsman office within
   15 days of receiving the resolution. If still unsatisfied, the customer
   can escalate externally to the National Bank of Romania or the Romanian
   Alternative Dispute Resolution entity for the banking sector (SAL-Fin).

Complaints related to suspected fraud follow an expedited version of this
process and should always be reported by phone first, since card blocking
may be needed immediately.
