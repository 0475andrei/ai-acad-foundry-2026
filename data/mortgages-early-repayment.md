---
title: Mortgage Early Repayment Terms
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-01
version: 2
---

Customers may repay part or all of a Libra Bank mortgage before the end of
its term at any time, subject to the following conditions.

**During the fixed-rate period** (the first 5 years of a Libra Fixed
Mortgage), an early repayment fee of **1% of the amount repaid early**
applies. This fee compensates the bank for the interest rate risk it has
locked in on the customer's behalf.

**During the variable-rate period** (Libra Variable Mortgage from day one,
or a Libra Fixed Mortgage after its first 5 years), no early repayment fee
applies. Customers can repay any amount at any time without penalty.

To make an early repayment, the customer must submit a request through the
mobile app or at a branch at least 5 business days before the desired
repayment date. The customer can choose to either reduce the monthly
payment while keeping the original term, or keep the monthly payment the
same and shorten the term.

Partial early repayments below 5,000 RON are not accepted; the minimum
partial early repayment amount is 5,000 RON. There is no minimum for a full
early repayment that closes the loan entirely.
