Current annual interest rates for Libra Bank deposit products, effective
from 1 February 2026.

| Term | Libra Fixed Deposit | Libra Flexible Deposit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 month | 3.2% | 2.6% |
| 3 months | 3.8% | 3.1% |
| 6 months | 4.3% | 3.5% |
| 12 months | 4.7% | 3.9% |
| 24 months | 4.5% | 3.7% |

Libra Savings Account currently pays 2.9% annual interest, calculated daily
on the end-of-day balance and paid monthly to the linked current account.

Rates for Libra Fixed Deposit and Libra Flexible Deposit are locked for the
full term at the rate in effect on the day the deposit is opened. The Libra
Savings Account rate is variable and reviewed monthly; the rate shown here
can change at any time with 30 days' notice, as described in the deposits
overview document.

Interest on all deposit products is subject to standard Romanian withholding
tax, deducted automatically before payout.
