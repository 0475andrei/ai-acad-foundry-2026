To apply for a Libra Bank credit card, an applicant must meet the following
criteria:

- Be at least 18 years old and a Romanian resident.
- Hold a current account with Libra Bank for at least 30 days, or open one as
  part of the application.
- Have a documented net monthly income of at least 2,500 RON for Libra Credit
  Classic, or at least 6,000 RON for Libra Credit Gold.
- Have no active default flags in the national credit bureau (Biroul de
  Credit) at the time of application.
- For Libra Credit Gold specifically, have at least one year of continuous
  credit history with any bank in Romania.

Self-employed applicants must provide the last two years of tax returns
instead of a payslip. Applicants who do not meet the income threshold for
Libra Credit Gold are automatically considered for Libra Credit Classic
instead, unless they opt out.

Approval also depends on the applicant's debt-to-income ratio, which must not
exceed 40% including the requested card's minimum payment estimate. The
annual fee and other charges for approved cards are listed in the card fees
document for the current year, not in this document.
