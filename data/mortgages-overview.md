Libra Bank offers two mortgage products, both denominated in RON only:
**Libra Fixed Mortgage** and **Libra Variable Mortgage**.

Libra Fixed Mortgage locks the interest rate for the first 5 years of the
loan, after which it converts to a variable rate tied to ROBOR plus a fixed
margin. Libra Variable Mortgage uses a variable rate from day one, tied to
ROBOR plus a fixed margin that is typically lower than the fixed-period
margin.

Both products support terms from 5 to 30 years and can finance up to 85% of
the property value (loan-to-value, or LTV) for a primary residence, or up to
75% LTV for a second home or investment property.

Mortgages can be used to purchase an existing property, purchase a property
under construction, or refinance a mortgage held at another bank. Property
insurance covering the full replacement value is mandatory for the life of
the loan and can be arranged through Libra Bank's insurance partner or an
external insurer approved by the bank.

For eligibility requirements, see the mortgage eligibility document. For
current rates and fees, see the mortgage rates and fees document. For early
repayment terms, see the mortgage early repayment document.
