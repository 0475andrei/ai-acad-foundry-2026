Libra Bank offers three retail card products: Libra Debit, Libra Credit Classic,
and Libra Credit Gold.

**Libra Debit** is issued automatically with any current account. It draws
directly from the account balance, has no credit limit, and carries no annual
fee.

**Libra Credit Classic** is an entry-level credit card aimed at customers with
a stable income and no prior credit history requirement beyond a basic income
check. It carries a small annual fee and a standard interest rate on revolving
balances.

**Libra Credit Gold** is a premium credit card offering travel insurance,
airport lounge access twice a year, and a higher credit limit. It requires a
minimum monthly income and at least one year of credit history with any bank.

All cards are issued in RON and can be used for contactless payments, online
purchases, and ATM withdrawals. Cards are valid for 4 years from issue and are
reissued automatically before expiry unless the customer closes the account.

For eligibility requirements, see the card eligibility document. For the
current fee schedule, see the 2026 card fees document.
