The Libra Current Account is the base retail account required to hold any
Libra Bank card, deposit, or mortgage product. It is available in RON and
comes with a Libra Debit card at no extra cost.

Opening a Libra Current Account requires a valid Romanian ID, proof of
address no older than 3 months, and can be completed entirely through the
mobile app for existing Romanian residents, or at a branch for other cases.

Monthly maintenance fee is 0 RON for customers who receive their salary via
the account or maintain a minimum balance of 1,000 RON; otherwise a 9
RON/month fee applies. Domestic transfers between Libra Bank accounts are
free and instant. Domestic transfers to other Romanian banks are free via
the mobile app and 5 RON at a branch. International transfers are priced
separately and are out of scope for this document.

Account statements are available monthly through the app at no charge; a
paper statement mailed to the customer's address costs 10 RON per request.
