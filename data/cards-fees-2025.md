This document lists the annual and transaction fees for Libra Bank card
products, effective from 1 January 2025 through 31 December 2025.

| Card | Annual fee | Cash withdrawal fee (Libra ATM) | Cash withdrawal fee (other ATM) | Foreign transaction fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Libra Debit | 0 RON | 0 RON | 5 RON | 1.5% |
| Libra Credit Classic | 60 RON | 0 RON | 8 RON | 2% |
| Libra Credit Gold | 250 RON | 0 RON | 8 RON | 1% |

Late payment fee on credit cards is 50 RON per missed minimum payment. Card
replacement due to loss or theft costs 30 RON; replacement due to a
manufacturing defect is free.

This schedule was superseded by the 2026 fee schedule. Where the two
documents disagree, the document with the later effective date applies.
