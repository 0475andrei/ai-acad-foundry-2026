Current rates and fees for Libra Bank mortgage products, effective from 1
January 2026.

| Product | Rate (fixed period) | Rate (variable, after fixed period or from day one) | Arrangement fee | Property valuation fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Libra Fixed Mortgage | 6.2% for years 1-5 | ROBOR + 2.3% after year 5 | 0.5% of loan amount, min. 500 RON | 800 RON |
| Libra Variable Mortgage | n/a | ROBOR + 1.9% from day one | 0.3% of loan amount, min. 400 RON | 800 RON |

The arrangement fee is charged once, at disbursement, and is not refundable
if the loan is repaid early. The property valuation fee is charged upfront,
before the credit decision, and is non-refundable regardless of the outcome
of the application.

A mandatory life insurance policy, priced separately based on the
borrower's age and loan amount, is required for the duration of the loan
and is not included in the fees above.

Rates shown are for applicants who meet the eligibility criteria described
in the mortgage eligibility document. Applicants with a loan-to-value above
75% are charged an additional 0.3% margin on top of the rates shown above.
