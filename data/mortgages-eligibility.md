To apply for a Libra Bank mortgage, an applicant must meet the following
criteria:

- Be at least 21 years old at application and no older than 65 at the
  scheduled final payment date.
- Have a documented net monthly household income of at least 4,000 RON.
- Provide a down payment covering at least 15% of the property value for a
  primary residence (25% for a second home or investment property).
- Have no active default flags in the national credit bureau at the time of
  application.
- Pass Libra Bank's debt-to-income assessment: total monthly debt
  obligations, including the new mortgage payment, must not exceed 45% of
  net household income.

Applicants who are self-employed must provide the last three years of tax
returns and a business activity certificate. Co-borrowers are allowed and
their combined income is used for the debt-to-income calculation.

Approval is also subject to a property valuation performed by a Libra Bank
approved appraiser, at the applicant's cost. The interest rate and fees that
apply to an approved mortgage are set out in the mortgage rates and fees
document for the relevant effective date, not in this document.
