**Opening a deposit.** A Libra Fixed Deposit or Libra Flexible Deposit can be
opened through the mobile app, online banking, or at any branch. Funds are
transferred from the customer's current account and are unavailable for
other uses for the chosen term.

**Renewal.** By default, both deposit types renew automatically at maturity
for the same term, at whatever interest rate is in effect on the renewal
date. Customers can disable automatic renewal at any time before maturity,
in which case the principal and interest are transferred to the linked
current account at maturity.

**Early withdrawal from a Fixed Deposit.** Withdrawing before maturity is
allowed but forfeits all interest accrued for the current term; only the
original principal is returned. There is no additional penalty fee beyond
the loss of interest.

**Early withdrawal from a Flexible Deposit.** Partial withdrawals are
allowed at any time without forfeiting interest already accrued on the
remaining balance, but a minimum balance of 500 RON must be maintained to
keep the deposit open. Withdrawing the full balance closes the deposit and
pays out interest accrued up to that date at the Flexible Deposit rate.

**Deposit guarantee.** All Libra Bank deposits, including Libra Savings
Account balances, are covered by the Romanian Bank Deposit Guarantee Fund up
to the legal limit per depositor per bank, regardless of the number of
individual deposit accounts held.
