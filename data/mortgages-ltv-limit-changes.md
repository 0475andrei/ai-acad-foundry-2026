This document tracks changes to Libra Bank's maximum loan-to-value (LTV)
limit for mortgages on a primary residence.

**Before 15 January 2026:** the maximum LTV for a primary residence was 80%,
meaning a minimum down payment of 20% was required.

**From 15 January 2026:** the maximum LTV for a primary residence was raised
to 85%, meaning a minimum down payment of 15% is now required. This change
followed an internal risk policy update and applies to all applications
submitted on or after 15 January 2026. Applications submitted before that
date, but not yet disbursed, were evaluated under the old 80% limit unless
the customer explicitly requested re-evaluation under the new limit.

The maximum LTV for a second home or investment property was not changed by
this update and remains 75%, as stated in the mortgage overview document.

Where this document and the mortgage overview document appear to disagree
on the primary residence LTV limit, this document is authoritative, since it
reflects the most recent policy change and includes the effective date.
