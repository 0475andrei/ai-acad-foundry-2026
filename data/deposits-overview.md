Libra Bank offers three term deposit products: **Libra Fixed Deposit**,
**Libra Flexible Deposit**, and **Libra Savings Account**.

**Libra Fixed Deposit** locks funds for a chosen term (1, 3, 6, 12, or 24
months) at a fixed interest rate, paid at maturity. Early withdrawal is
allowed but forfeits the accrued interest for that term, as described in the
deposit terms and conditions document.

**Libra Flexible Deposit** allows partial withdrawals during the term
without losing all accrued interest, in exchange for a lower interest rate
than the equivalent Fixed Deposit term.

**Libra Savings Account** has no fixed term and no withdrawal restrictions,
with interest calculated daily and paid monthly. Its rate is variable and
can change at any time with 30 days' notice to the customer.

All deposit products are available to individual customers with an active
Libra Bank current account, in amounts starting from 500 RON. Deposits are
covered by the Romanian Bank Deposit Guarantee Fund up to the legal limit
per depositor per bank.

For current interest rates by term, see the deposit interest rates document.
