This document lists the annual and transaction fees for Libra Bank card
products, effective from 1 January 2026. It replaces the 2025 fee schedule.

| Card | Annual fee | Cash withdrawal fee (Libra ATM) | Cash withdrawal fee (other ATM) | Foreign transaction fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Libra Debit | 0 RON | 0 RON | 6 RON | 1.5% |
| Libra Credit Classic | 70 RON | 0 RON | 9 RON | 2% |
| Libra Credit Gold | 250 RON | 0 RON | 9 RON | 0.5% |

The foreign transaction fee for Libra Credit Gold was reduced from 1% to
0.5% starting 1 January 2026, as part of a promotional benefit for premium
cardholders. All other terms carry over unchanged from 2025 unless listed
above.

Late payment fee on credit cards remains 50 RON per missed minimum payment.
Card replacement due to loss or theft remains 30 RON; replacement due to a
manufacturing defect remains free.
